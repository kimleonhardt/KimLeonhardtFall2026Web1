History of Web Art

Characteristics of early internet art

  • Immateriality and Virtual Existence
    • Early internet art required the viewer to become a participant, often transforming passive browsing into active engagement. Works were rarely static; they were designed to be clicked, manipulated, or altered by the user, making the audience a part of the creative process.
  • Networkability and Connectivity
    • Unlike traditional art, early internet art often existed only in the digital realm, defying physicality and traditional gallery structures. It lived as code on servers, HTML, and browser windows, emphasizing a "non-object" or ephemeral quality.
  • Use of "Web 1.0" Aesthetics and Code
    • Artists often used the raw tools of the early internet—basic HTML code, browser frames, glitch aesthetics, and early graphic user interfaces—as their medium. Examples include the chaotic, anti-aesthetic, and deconstructive work of collective JODI, which treated code and error messages as art.
  • Subversion of Institutions and Commercialism
    • Early internet art often held a "utopian," democratic ethos, aiming to bypass traditional art galleries, museums, and market structures. It was often created by enthusiasts and artists operating outside conventional commercial art systems.

Early Web Artists

5 Artist 150 Character Description of Work LINKED THUMBNAIL
Olia Lialina Olia was an artist and web developer in the 1990s that used the internet as a space for self expression and artistic storytelling. She is also a key voice in contemporary discussions about art and new media due to her work in “net language.”
Auriea Harvey Auria Harvey is known for her work in art video and video game art. Her interest in sketching and painting go beyond a physical canvas and she has even created art through photoshop. In 1995 Harvey Entropy 8 an interactive website that experiments with the potential of net art.
Antoni Muntadas Antoni Muntadas is a multimedia artist that works with media such as photography, video and text. He is also the creator of The File Room in 1994. It was a web browser where people could search up dates, terms and events and see what comes up.
Shirin Kouladjie Shirin Kouladjie is an artist that has been living in Canada since 1998 which is also the time she decided to combine her two passions, mathematics and art. She chooses to work with everyday images she finds on the internet such as pictures from television and magazines.
Douglas Davis Douglas Davis is one of the earliest authors to post art work on the World Wide Web in 1994. It was displayed on his own website called, The World's First Collaborative Sentence. It included essays and pieces of critical writing from authors such as Susan Hoeltzel.
