Inside NYC's Subways

main

A phenomenon on the New York City subway system: South American women who carry their infant on their back and sell candy from a tray.

There is a lot of judgment coming from other communities who see them as bad mothers.

Western idea of good motherhood which mostly confines good mothers to the domestic space and typically separates labor and childcare.
