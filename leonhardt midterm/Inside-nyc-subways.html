SUBWAY

The treatment of migrant candy sellers also belongs to a longer history of immigrant labor and public-space regulation in New York City. Street vending has long provided people with little resources a way to earn income and to serve their communities. At the same time, vendors have been criticized for congestion, sanitation concerns, competition with established businesses, and their use of shared public space.
