History of Women bringing children to work

What is expected

  • Caregiving
    • While many candy-selling mothers who bring their children on the subway have no other choice because of missing childcare options, the practice is more than a logistical and economic necessity.
  • Motherhood
    • In many South American indigenous and rural communities it’s a cultural tradition for mothers to carry their children while working which allows them to move around and work while also keeping their children safe
  • Interviews
    • Interviews of the women themselves or New Yorkers who have seen them or interacted with them on the train. This would be done with a participatory style of documentary.
